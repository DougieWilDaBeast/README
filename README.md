## Hi, I'm Will 👋

I'm an electronic engineer who builds AI that runs on real hardware. I've spent five years at Intel in Ireland, first in hardware verification and now on the NPU (the AI chip inside Copilot+ PCs). I designed and built the demo platform that runs AI workloads side by side on the NPU, CPU and GPU, and I've triaged around 150 third-party AI apps for NPU support.

Outside work I build things, break them and build them again: a tactical RPG, an offline voice assistant, a knowledge base that an AI agent keeps tidy, and a to-do app heading for Google Play.

📍 Based in Ireland, moving to **Brisbane, Australia in February 2027**.
